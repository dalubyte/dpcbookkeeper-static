Pricing FAQ

The questions we get asked most.

Why is the price a "starting at" range?
Because a single-physician DPC with one bank account is genuinely different from a three-doc practice with two entities and a payroll of eight. The starting price is the floor for clean, simple practices. We quote a flat monthly fee on the discovery call once we know your shape — and that's what you pay, period.
What does the setup fee actually cover?
Building your DPC-specific chart of accounts, integrating your membership platform and bank feeds, cleaning the current year-to-date so we start from a known-good baseline, and onboarding you to our workflow. It is not a charge to "start the relationship" — it's the foundation that makes the monthly cadence possible.
Can I change tiers later?
Yes — most practices do. People often start at Starter or Core and step up to Growth or Full Service as the practice grows. There is no penalty to move tiers and we'll tell you when you're outgrowing your current one.
Is there a contract or minimum term?
Month-to-month after setup. We don't lock anyone in. If we're not the right fit, you should be able to leave — and we'll hand off cleanly.
Do you bill hourly for anything?
No. Monthly tiers are flat. Add-ons like catch-up bookkeeping or prior-year returns are quoted as flat projects. You'll always know what something costs before we start.
What if my practice is bigger than Full Service?
Multi-location, multiple entities, or a practice doing real M&A — let's talk. We'll scope a custom engagement that still feels like flat monthly pricing, not consultant hours.
