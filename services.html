General small business

Your brother’s restaurant needs its own bookkeeper. We only do DPC.

Same‑day emergencies

We’re fast, not an ER. Most things wait until morning.

FAQ

Questions
we keep getting.

Don’t see yours? Drop it in the form on the contact page. We answer every one.

Is my practice too small?
+
Probably not. Our smallest client opened last quarter with 60 members. We work with solo clinicians as often as four-provider groups. The flat fee scales down for solo practices.
Do you work with nurse practitioners and PAs?
+
Yes. Any DPC clinician — physicians, NPs, PAs. What matters is the membership model, not the letters after your name.
Can you work with multi‑state practices?
+
Yes. Several clients operate across state lines. Multi-state payroll, nexus, and returns are routine for us.
What if I’m still billing some insurance?
+
If it’s a small slice and you’re phasing out, we can work with you. If insurance is most of your revenue, we’re the wrong firm — we’ll refer you to a good generalist.
Do you offer a free consultation?
+
Yes. Thirty minutes, no pitch deck. If we’re not the right fit, we’ll say so. If we are, you’ll get a one-page engagement letter the next day.
What does onboarding cost?
+
If your books are clean, onboarding is included. If there’s a clean-up, we give you a flat-fee quote after a free review. No surprise invoices.
Who do I actually talk to?
+
One person. Same name, same email. Not a shared inbox, not a ticket queue. For now that’s Daniel. We only add team members when we can keep that relationship intact.
