BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few honest answers.

Is there a cost for this call?

No. Thirty minutes, free. Either we’re a fit and we start working together, or we tell you who to talk to instead.

What if I’m not actually a DPC — I do some DPC and some insurance?

We only take 100% DPC practices. If you’re hybrid, we’ll refer you to a generalist CPA who’s good with small medical practices.

Do you have a waitlist?

Usually. We cap the book at a number Daniel can handle personally. When we’re full, the call becomes a waitlist conversation — we’ll be straight about timing.

I’m pre‑launch. Is this too early?

It’s the best time. Entity selection, chart of accounts, QuickBooks setup — getting it right on day one saves two years of cleanup.
